Kwizzy is an unofficial player: it re-hosts questions locally for offline play and links back to each original quiz. Memory lives in your browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.

Your memory

A Answered before badge on a question means it already lives in your browser memory. Random 20 draws unseen questions first, then tops up with badged ones.

A Last played 3 days ago stamp — on a quiz, a Category, a question and the score line — is the date of the last time you played it; a quiz, Category or question with no stamp has never been played. Last times live in your browser too, and Reset memory erases them.
